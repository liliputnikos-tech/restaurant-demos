Coffee shop · Acton Town station

Good coffee, lovely cakes, right by the station.

The Apple Tree is a friendly little coffee shop on Station Parade, a few steps from Acton Town Underground station. Pop in for a coffee on your way to the train, a quick lunch, or a slice of something sweet.

Open seven days a week
