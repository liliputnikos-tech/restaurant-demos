Coffee Bakery Balkan Lux is a family-run bakery and coffee shop in Acton, serving traditional Balkan bakes: crisp, flaky burek with cheese or meat, fresh pastries and a proper cup of coffee.

See the bakes07946 051555

A taste of home, straight from the oven.

Balkan Lux brings the bakery traditions of the Balkans to Churchfield Road. The star of the counter is burek, filo pastry baked until golden, with a generous filling, crisp outside and soft within.

Pop in for breakfast, grab something warm to take home, or stay a while with a coffee. Takeaway cups are compostable, too.

From our ovens

Burek

The house speciality. Flaky filo pastry baked with cheese or with meat.

Pizza burek

Flaky burek pastry with pizza-style toppings. Best ordered in advance.

A traditional boat-shaped pie. Best ordered in advance.

Sweet and savoury bakes, including chocolate-filled croissants.

Freshly made, to enjoy in the café or to take away.
