Egyptian cooking,

Egyptian House sits on Bloemfontein Road in White City, W12. It’s an easy-going place to eat, catch up with friends and take your time.

The kitchen cooks Egyptian food in a generous, homely style. Don’t miss the falafel, made the Egyptian way.
