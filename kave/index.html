About Kave

A small café with a big plant-based heart.

Pull up a seat for a morning coffee, a slow brunch or a quiet hour with a book. Kave makes everyday favourites alongside a generous choice of vegan dishes, drinks and bakes, so everyone at the table is sorted.

  • Vegan & vegetarian options
  • Brunch
  • Dogs welcome
  • Eat in or take away
